# Qualitative source bundle

The three PNG files are exact copies selected by the audited final-AMPT
visualization manifest. `qualitative_manifest.csv` replaces the original
machine-specific absolute paths with repository-relative paths. The asset
generator hashes the manifest and all three images before producing the contact
sheet.

These images illustrate the final full-benchmark policy only. They are not
members of, and provide no statistics for, the historical 658-scene recovery
comparison (367 versus 440).
